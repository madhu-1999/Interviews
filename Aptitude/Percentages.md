# % comparison
1.  A is what % of B ? 
$$= \frac{A}{B}\times100$$
>[!example]
>32 is what % of 80
>$=\frac{32}{80}\times100=40\%$

2. A is what % less than B ? 
$$= \frac{(B-A)}{B}\times100$$
3. B is what % more than A ?
$$= \frac{B-A}{A}\times100$$
# Successive % change
$$\pm x \space\pm y\space \pm \frac{xy}{100}$$
>[!example]
>40% increase then 20% discount
> $= +40 - 20 - \frac{800}{100} = 20-8 = 12\%$
> <br/>
> 20% increase then another 20% increase
> $= 20 + 20  + \frac{400}{100} = 40 + 4 = 44\%$
