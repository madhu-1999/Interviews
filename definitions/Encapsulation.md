Encapsulation is the practice of __bundling data (variables) and behaviors (functions) together__ into a single unit (class) while __restricting direct access__ to some of the object's components. (data hiding)
+ [p] Protects data from accidental corruption and ensures control via getters/setters.

> [!example] 
> A bank account hides the balance variable; you must use `deposit()` or `withdraw()` to update it.
