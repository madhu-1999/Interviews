# What is Big Data?
It is characterized by 5V's:
## Volume
It refers to the quantity (ex: 1M records) and the size (ex: 1 TB) of the data.
>[!example]
>- Facebook generates **~4 PB** of new data daily
>- NYSE generates **~1 TB** of trade data daily
## Variety
It refers to the diversity and range of different data types, typically divided into 3 categories:
**Structured Data**: Relational DB's, Spreadsheets, CSV Files, Fixed Schemas
**Unstructured Data**: Text docs, Images, Audio, Video
**Semi-structured Data**: Logs, JSON, XML, Key-value stores
## Velocity
It refers to the speed at which data is received, stored and managed from its source.
>[!example]
>- Stock trades: millions per second.
>- IoT sensors: continuous streams, thousands of events/second per fleet.
>- E-commerce: thousands of transactions per minute at peak.


## Veracity
It refers to the credibility and reliability of the data. 
Common quality problems: 
+ inconsistent formats (dates, addresses), 
+ missing values 
+ duplicate records
+ stale information
+ measurement error
+ outright fraud.
## Value
It refers to the analysis, insights, discoveries and ultimately business decisions and consumer outcomes that result from the data collected.
